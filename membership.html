How It Works

Three pillars. One cohesive program.

Every month, all three pillars run on a shared theme, so nothing feels like a separate program bolted on. Training, nutrition, and mindset work together, not in parallel.

01

Strength Training

Delivered via TrainHeroic · 4 days per week · Foundation or Build Track
  • A new 4-day programming block every month, with progressive overload built in from month to month.
  • Two tracks to suit your experience: Foundation (building from the ground up with machines and dumbbells) or Build (structured progressive overload with compound barbell movements).
  • You're placed in the right track during onboarding, based on your intake form, not a generic quiz.
  • Every session includes coaching cues and home gym modifications so you always know what you're doing and why.
  • Straight sets for compound movements, supersets for accessories, efficient sessions designed for real schedules.
  • Full gym or home gym, the program adapts to what you have access to.
02

Evidence-Based Nutrition

From an MNU Certified Nutritionist · No meal plans · No food rules
  • Your Nutrition Foundations Guide is included on day one: a comprehensive, research-backed reference covering energy balance, protein, hunger hormones, your menstrual cycle, gut health, and supplementation.
  • Biweekly nutrition content: a teaching video in Week 1 and a practical resource (PDF, swap guide, or walkthrough) in Week 3.
  • A progressive 6-month curriculum, with topics building on each other month by month, from foundations to optimisation.
  • Everything is evidence-based, grounded in peer-reviewed research, not trends. When the science is mixed, you'll be told that too.
  • The goal is understanding, not compliance. Women who understand why make better decisions than women who just follow rules.
03

Mindset & Lifestyle Coaching

Weekly · Voice notes · Community prompts
  • A 5 to 8 minute voice note or short video every week on the themes that actually determine long-term results: identity, consistency, stress, body image, recovery, and sustainability.
  • Monthly themes that tie all three pillars together, so you're not just training and eating better, you're thinking differently about both.
  • Weekly community reflection prompts to create space for the honest conversations most fitness programs skip entirely.
  • This is the layer that keeps women in the program when motivation dips, life gets hard, and results feel slow. It's not optional, it's the glue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered.

How much time does this require each week?

Training is 4 days per week, and sessions are designed to take 50 to 60 minutes each. Beyond that, the nutrition and mindset content is delivered in short, practical formats (10 to 15 minute videos and 5 to 8 minute voice notes) and the two monthly calls total about 1 hour 45 minutes. The community is there whenever you want it, but it's not somewhere you need to be constantly. Most members spend 30 to 60 minutes per week on content outside of training.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this suitable for me?

Yes. The Foundation Track is specifically designed for women who are new to structured training, returning after a significant break, or not yet confident in the gym. Movements are dumbbell and machine-based, every exercise includes a coaching cue and a home gym modification, and the programming builds gradually. You don't need any prior experience to start.

I've been training for years. Is this advanced enough?

The Build Track is designed for women with 1+ years of consistent training who are comfortable with compound barbell movements and ready for structured progressive overload. If you're already training regularly and looking for smarter programming rather than just harder programming, yes, this is designed for you.

Do I need a full gym, or can I train at home?

Both. Every session in TrainHeroic includes a home gym modification for every exercise. The Foundation Track is primarily dumbbell and machine-based, which means many sessions can be done with just dumbbells. The Build Track is designed around a full gym but all barbell movements have dumbbell alternatives listed. If you have access to dumbbells and a bench, you can make either track work at home.

What app do I use for training?

Training is delivered through TrainHeroic, a dedicated strength coaching app. You'll need to download it (free) and you'll receive instructions to join your track during onboarding. It's straightforward to use and has a great logging feature that lets you track weights and leave notes on sessions.

Where does the community and content live?

Everything outside of training lives in Skool, your home base for the community, nutrition and mindset content, call replays, and the program calendar. It's accessible on desktop and via the Skool app. TrainHeroic handles training. Two apps total, that's it.

I've tried group programs before and felt lost. How is this different?

The onboarding experience is designed specifically to prevent that. You complete an intake form, you're placed in the right training track, and you receive a welcome video, a 3-part email sequence, and a personalised welcome message in the community, all in your first week. As a founding member, you also receive a complimentary 1:1 call with Meghan to personalise your approach from the start. The group is intentionally kept to 20 members so it never becomes anonymous.

I can't always make the live calls. Does that matter?

Not at all. Both calls are recorded and replays are posted in Skool within 24 hours. Many members watch the replays more than they attend live, and the value is the same either way. The only thing you miss by not attending live is the real-time Q&A, but questions posted in the community after the fact always get answered.

How is the nutrition guidance structured? Do I have to track macros?

No. The nutrition framework is evidence-based but flexible, designed around understanding and habit-building rather than rigid tracking. You'll receive a comprehensive Nutrition Foundations Guide from day one, biweekly nutrition content that builds month by month, and coaching on calls that addresses your specific situation. Whether you want to track or not, there's a practical approach that works for you. Tracking is presented as a tool, not a requirement.

I have a complicated relationship with food. Is this a safe space for that?

The Embodied Method is not a clinical program and is not a replacement for professional support if you're dealing with disordered eating or an eating disorder. If that applies to you, please speak with a qualified professional first. For women who have had difficult relationships with food in the past and are looking for a healthy, evidence-based approach that doesn't promote restriction or food fear, yes, this is specifically designed to move away from that.
