Meet your coach

Hi, I'm Meghan.

Strength. Fuel. Freedom.

For over a decade, I lived in the restrict, binge, and start-over cycle, convinced that if I just had more discipline I'd finally get it right. I didn't. Everything changed when I stopped training to shrink and started training to feel strong.

Now I help women in their 30s and 40s do the same. Not through restriction or punishment, but through real strength training, nutrition you actually understand, and the mindset to make it last. Because health was never meant to feel like a fight. It's supposed to feel like coming home.
