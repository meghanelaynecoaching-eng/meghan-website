My Story

It started with one word.

I grew up in the 90s worshipping Barbie, coming of age when "heroin chic" was aspirational. But my body shame wasn't just shaped by culture, it came from home too. When I was 14, a single offhand comment planted a seed: there's something wrong with me.

For the next decade, I tried to fix myself. I restricted, I binged, I counted and obsessed and started over, endlessly. I even built my career around it, certain that more knowledge would finally give me control. It didn't. In my late 20s, years of restriction caught up with me, and my body rebelled.

"It felt like the universe was forcing me to love myself in my most feared form.
So I listened."

What I Believe

The industry sells suffering.

Instead of training to shrink, I started training to perform. I chased strength, not the scale. I learned to fuel my body, studied sleep, and genuinely started taking care of myself.

When you focus on what your body can do, the aesthetic results follow. But more than that, you stop hating the process. You stop starting over every Monday. You stop living in shame.

Health isn't supposed to feel like punishment.
It's supposed to feel like coming home.

Who I Work With

Smart, capable, successful,
except here.

I work with women in their 30s and 40s who have tried the bootcamps, the detoxes, the tracking apps, and are still stuck in the all-or-nothing cycle. Exhausted, not from lack of effort, but from years of effort in the wrong direction.

"I don't give you a plan to follow.
I teach you how to live differently."

My Philosophy

suffer to change.

You don't need to be perfect, you need to be consistent, and consistency only comes when the process doesn't feel like deprivation. Train for performance. Fuel for strength. Build a life that doesn't require a "fresh start" every Monday.
