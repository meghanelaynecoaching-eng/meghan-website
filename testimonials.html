Featured Client Story

"Trust her process, because it works."

After 8 months of training on my own with no results, I was referred to Meghan. She was described as "no nonsense, and will hold you accountable," and that resonated. I wanted someone who actually cared, who could teach me how to eat properly rather than just hand me a meal plan, and who could build workouts I'd enjoy. I've always hated the gym and despised cardio. Meghan found exercises I could enjoy and see progress on, exactly what I wanted.

As I went through her program, my relationship with food changed. I began to see it as fuel. There was a moment where I told her "I can't eat like this forever," and she said "don't think about doing it forever, just think about doing it tomorrow." She was right. After a few months I stopped tracking and started trusting my own judgement, and I've had continued success since. I don't deprive myself of the foods I enjoy; I fit them into my life in a healthy way.

The success stopped being about numbers and became about how I felt in my body. My posture improved, stairs and hills no longer took my breath away, my sleep improved. There are still things I'd like to improve, women always find something, but I feel more confident now than I have in years. Now I feel strong.

I was nervous to invest in six months of virtual coaching, but the programs made it easy, and Meghan was supportive, encouraging, and knowledgeable throughout. She wasn't pushy. She dug deep to find out if we'd be a good fit, and I'm grateful she took a chance on me, because the experience changed me. If you're serious about a real lifestyle change, I'd wholeheartedly recommend her. Do what she says, and you'll have success. Trust her process, because it works.

— Amy Fraleigh

Meet Your Coach

I didn't just learn this. I lived it.

Left: a woman who was lost, desperate to figure it out, and convinced that if she just looked different she'd finally feel okay. Right: a woman who stopped chasing a look and found something so much better.

These photos are six years apart. But the real distance between them isn't measured in time. It's measured in mindset.

The woman on the left was doing everything she thought she was supposed to do. She wanted to lose weight so badly. She thought that was the answer. That if she could just get there (wherever "there" was) she'd feel like herself. She never got there. Because "there" didn't exist.

What changed everything wasn't a new diet. It wasn't more discipline. It wasn't finally finding the willpower she thought she was missing. It was weight training. And eating enough to actually fuel it.

The moment I stopped training to shrink and started training to perform, everything shifted. I got stronger. I started eating more, not less. I stopped dreading the gym and started looking forward to it. And somewhere in the middle of all of that, I stopped hating my body and started being genuinely in awe of what it could do.

The physical changes came. They always do. But they were never the real win. The real win was becoming a woman who feels at home in her body. Who trains because she loves what she's capable of. Who eats to fuel her life, not to punish herself for living it.

If you're the woman on the left right now, I see you. I was you. And I promise you there's another way.

MeghanElayne
