The Shift

You're not stuck because you're unmotivated.

You're stuck because you've been trying to force results with systems that were never built around your life. Sound familiar?

  • You're doing "all the right things" but not seeing consistent results
  • Stuck in cycles of dieting, overeating, under-eating, or burnout
  • Confused about how much to eat, how to train, or what actually matters
  • Struggling with body image, guilt around food, or all-or-nothing thinking

01

02

Monthly Check-In Calls

A 30-minute call each month to review progress, adjust your nutrition and training, and work through any mindset or lifestyle barriers.

03

Weekly Online Check-Ins

Through the coaching app, we review your habits and progress each week and make thoughtful adjustments, so you're never guessing what's next.

04

Ongoing 1:1 Support

Direct access to me between check-ins for questions, support through the hard moments, and accountability when motivation dips. Real coaching, not just a plan.

Nutrition Coaching & Strategy

A personalized, flexible approach: structure and guidance without obsession, and tools that support consistency, not perfection.

06

Customized Training Program
