What It Is

A program built around your real life, not an ideal one.

You don't need another challenge or meal plan. The Embodied Method is a monthly practice that brings strength training, evidence-based nutrition, and mindset coaching together, so the results actually last past Monday.

Strength Training

A new 4-day programming block every month, delivered via TrainHeroic, with a track to match your experience and coaching cues in every session.

Evidence-Based Nutrition

No meal plans, no food rules. Just a clear, research-backed way to understand how to fuel your body, from an MNU Certified Nutritionist.

Mindset & Lifestyle

Weekly coaching on the things that actually determine whether it lasts: consistency, stress, body image, and sustainability.

A Quick Look

What's inside, every month.

  • Monthly strength training programming with two tracks to suit your level
  • Evidence-based nutrition guidance and a foundations guide to keep
  • Weekly mindset coaching and an active, moderated community
  • Two group coaching calls a month, with replays
  • Personalised onboarding so you start in the right place
